# Northwind Supply — Customer Handbook

## Returns and refunds

Refunds are processed within 5 working days of the item arriving back at our
warehouse. The money returns to the original payment method; we cannot refund
to a different card or account.

Items must be unused and in their original packaging to be eligible for return.
Opened consumables, cut-to-length cable and custom-fabricated parts cannot be
returned unless they arrived faulty.

You have 30 days from delivery to start a return. After 30 days we can only
help if the item is covered by its manufacturer warranty.

To start a return, email returns@northwind.example with your order number. We
send a prepaid label within one working day. Do not send anything back without
a label — unlabelled parcels are refused at the depot.

## Delivery

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. Express delivery arrives the next
working day if ordered before 2pm.

Delivery is free on orders over £75. Below that, standard delivery is £4.95 and
express is £11.95.

We deliver to mainland UK addresses. We do not currently ship to the Channel
Islands, the Isle of Man, or outside the UK.

Tracking numbers are emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse, not when the
order is placed. There is usually a few hours' gap.

## Accounts

You can change the email address on your account from the Settings page. The
change takes effect immediately, but invoices already issued keep the old
address for accounting reasons.

Trade accounts get 30-day payment terms and tiered pricing. To apply, you need
a company registration number and two trade references.

If you are locked out, use the reset link on the sign-in page. Support cannot
reset a password over the phone — it always goes through email.

## Warehouse and collection

Our warehouse is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, and Saturday 9am to 1pm.
It is closed on bank holidays.

Click and collect orders are ready within two hours during opening times. We
hold collections for 7 days before returning them to stock.

The trade counter closes 30 minutes before the warehouse does.

## Damaged or missing items

Report damage within 48 hours of delivery, with photographs of the item and the
outer packaging. We cannot process a damage claim without a photograph of the
packaging.

If a parcel is marked delivered but you do not have it, wait 24 hours before
reporting it — couriers occasionally scan early. After 24 hours, contact
support and we will open an investigation with the courier.

Missing items from a multi-parcel order are usually a split shipment. Check the
tracking for a second parcel before reporting a shortage.
